The elements shown in rectangular shapes represent the entities, while the elements shown in elliptical shapes represent the attributes of those entities. The relationships between the entities are represented using diamonds (or diamond-shaped boxes). The symbols connecting the entities to the relationships indicate their cardinality. In this model, the relationships appear to indicate that an entity can participate in zero or one instance of the relationship.
